# Stage 3 · C1 · Contrast and type-floor tokens (As built)

Stage 3, PR C1 (plan §8.3 "C1"; evidence SW-10, SW-11, L23, L24, X-02, SW-29,
N-17). Branch `feat/tw-s3-c1-contrast-tokens` from `0568d69` (the A1/B2/B1
stack). Date 2026-10-23. Stylesheets and tests only: no JavaScript, no
template markup, no words.

## What changes for users

Every page that loads the design system:

- **Brand text passes.** Text in the brand teal (links, codes, active labels,
  kickers, counts) uses `--text-brand`: 5.48:1 on a light card (teal was
  4.11:1) and the brighter mint in dark. Its aliases `--brand` and `--success`
  are the same failing colour and moved too, as did the lighter teals.
- **Filled primaries pass.** White on a teal fill was 4.11:1 (light) and
  3.04:1 (dark). The skip link, the sidebar's menu button and initials, and
  every teal-filled button, tab, step and badge now use the one primary pair:
  5.48:1 light, 8.13:1 dark, the hover darker (6.98:1), never brighter.
- **Warning text passes.** `--warning` text was 4.13:1 on the page in light;
  it is `--sev-warning` now (4.80:1; dark unchanged).
- **The faintest grey is no longer used for text.** `--t5` text (user
  management's "Never" and empty cells, 2.2:1; 541 of them on the QA copy)
  reads `--t4`.
- **Status pills pass** and use the severity colours. The workspace's "Draft"
  pill was 4.17:1; it is 5.15:1 light, 10.63:1 dark. In a toolbar a pill is
  flat, like every other toolbar surface.
- **Placeholders pass.** Every placeholder reads `--t4`: the workspace filter
  was 2.02:1 (light) and 1.95:1 (dark), now 4.82:1 and 4.73:1. On the QA copy,
  51 placeholders failed on 13 pages; none does now.
- **The sidebar never goes under 11px.** Its section labels measured 8.9px
  at 1440, 7.4px at 1100 and 6.5px in the phone drawer; links, the user's
  name and role, the initials, badges and the language menu were under 11px
  in the drawer too. All of it is on the type tokens now, at every width.
- **A light theme tells the browser it is light,** so a dark operating system
  no longer paints dark scrollbars, pickers and controls on a light page.
- **Forced colours:** the skip link keeps a visible box (pills already did).

Split view: its own stylesheet is untouched (stage 4). The one shared rule it
renders in brand text, the English heading, goes from 4.29:1 to 8.39:1 on its
card (light) and from 5.81:1 to 8.39:1 (dark). The MRI page: its own files are
untouched; the only change that reaches it on the QA copy is the skip link
(4.11:1 / 3.04:1 → 5.48:1 / 8.13:1).

## Deviations from the plan (with evidence)

1. **The fill colours have other names.** The plan counted `color:
   var(--teal)` (N-17). `--brand` and `--success` are the same `#0A8E6E`
   (4.11:1 on white), `--teal-bright` is 3.06:1 and `--teal-pale` 1.9:1;
   `--warning` is 4.13:1 on the page and `--t5` 2.2:1. A lint on `--teal` alone
   would let `color: var(--brand)`, the same failing colour, straight back in.
   All of them moved (table above), and the lint names every one, with or
   without a fallback, plus the literal copies dark rules made (`#0CA87E`,
   `#7a7e92`, …). `--teal-deep` is `--text-brand` in light and `--teal` in
   dark, so moving it changes only dark (brighter). `--brand-dark` is
   `--text-brand`'s own value in both themes and stays.
2. **Split: excluded and given a dark `--text-brand`, both.** Split's own 31
   fill-coloured text declarations are untouched (the lint counts them; the
   count may only fall). But the shared `.tw-english-heading` renders on split,
   and remapping it alone took it from 4.29:1 to 3.22:1 there in light (probe
   `audit/nosplittoken`). Split's token block now gives `--text-brand` its dark
   value, as the plan's alternative says. The plan's mutant "remap split →
   split audit red" is therefore **equivalent** (M4: remapping split's own teal
   text with the token in place *raises* it, 4.29 → 8.39:1); the mutant that
   guards the risk is "drop split's token" (M3, red).
3. **Split also declares `color-scheme: dark`.** The new light-theme scheme
   would otherwise give this always-dark page light native controls and
   popups under a light theme. The MRI page is also always dark and cannot be
   touched (owner): with an OS in dark mode and the app in light, its native
   controls and scrollbars are now light (before, the OS's dark leaked
   through); with an OS in light mode nothing changes there. Its scrollbars
   carry the design's own colours either way.
4. **Pills sit on an opaque 7% tint of the card,** not on `--sev-*-bg`. Those
   tints are for `--t1` text (global.css); `--sev-warning` on its own 9% tint
   over the page measured 4.21–4.46:1 (the Draft pill at 400px: 4.46:1). On
   the opaque tint each pair is a property of the tokens (light 4.91–5.88:1,
   dark 5.72–8.68:1, pinned) wherever the pill sits.
5. **Every placeholder, not only `.fb-search`.** The base rule and the six
   other overrides fixed 51 failing placeholders on 13 pages (QA); the browser's
   own grey measured 3.4–4.4:1.
6. **All sidebar text, not only its micro-labels.** In the phone drawer the
   links measured 10.1px, the name 10.5px, the badge is a fixed 9px; the
   acceptance ("sidebar labels at least 11px at 1440, 1100 and 400") holds
   only if they move too. Cost, measured (`sidebar_wrap.out`, before vs after,
   en/ar, page font and Verdana): at the §6 sizes (1366 and up) no link wraps;
   in the compact sidebar (≤1200px, 210px and 195px wide) 1–4 English and 2–7
   Arabic links take two lines (before: 0 and 1). No text is cut anywhere. A
   12px compact size was tried and saves at most one wrap, so no breakpoint
   sets a size.
7. **More fills than the sweep listed,** found by the rendered diff: the
   planner's active step number (white wash), the dark-only literal fills of
   the audit tabs (3.04:1) and the graduation tabs (4.11:1), bootstrap's
   `.text-bg-*` and `.progress-bar`, and the graph page's student node.
8. **Assets beyond global.css.** Every edited stylesheet is bumped: see Assets.
9. **The shared kicker rule.** global.css styles every page's kicker in one
   rule, which lists `.tw-mri-kicker` (the classic inspector's MRI tab). It
   moves from `--teal` to `--text-brand` with the others, as any global
   change reaches MRI (owner note, §8). `.twm-kicker` on the MRI page is
   coloured by its own sheet and does not change.
10. **"Split's teal text not lower than today"** is measured against split's
    own card, because split paints its page with a gradient (the audit's
    compositing cannot measure text on a gradient).
